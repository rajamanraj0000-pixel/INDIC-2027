Conference Schedule

Two-Day Intensive Academic Programme

Designed for deep interdisciplinary discourse, hands-on manuscript decipherment, and a special dawn expedition to Jaipur's iconic astronomical landmark.

01

Day 1: Foundations & Evidence

Friday, 02 April 2027 • Main Auditorium
In-Person + Hybrid
09:00 - 10:30

Inaugural Ceremony & Vedic Invocations

Welcome by UEM Chancellor, Keynote address by National IKS Division Leadership, Release of Conference Abstract Volume (with ISBN).

11:00 - 12:30

Plenary I: Mathematical Traditions of India

Keynote on Kerala School Calculus & Epistemic Foundations of Indian Proof (Upapatti) by leading historians of science.

13:30 - 15:30

Parallel Technical Sessions I & II

Tracks A (Mathematics), B (Astronomy) & C (Metallurgy & Engineering) paper presentations in designated halls.

15:45 - 17:30

Poster Session & Artifact Replica Exhibition

Interactive demonstration of working brass astrolabes, armillary spheres, and high-resolution manuscript facsimile display.

19:00 - 21:30

Rajasthani Folk Cultural Evening & Heritage Banquet

Gala dinner celebrating the living performing arts and royal culinary heritage of Rajasthan at the campus courtyard.

02

Day 2: Praxis, Jantar Mantar & Future

Saturday, 03 April 2027 • Field + Campus
Field Excursion Included
06:30 - 09:00

Guided Technical Visit to Jantar Mantar (UNESCO Site)

Special early morning solar azimuth and quadrant reading session at the Vrihat Samrat Yantra, curated by expert astronomers.

10:00 - 11:15

Plenary II: Transmission & Standards of Evidence

Keynote addressing historiographical methods, textual philology, and demarcating scientific evidence from speculation.

11:30 - 13:15

Hands-on Concurrent Workshops

Workshop A: Reading a Mathematical Manuscript (Transcription & Katapayadi).
Workshop B: Designing an Accredited IKS STEM Module under NEP 2020.

14:15 - 15:45

International Panel: "IKS in Higher Education: What is Actually Teachable?"

Deans, IIT Directors, AICTE representatives and international scholars debate pedagogy and lab-based curricula.

16:00 - 17:30

Valedictory, Best Paper Awards & Network MoU Signing

Official formalization of the International Network for Discourse on Indian Civilizational Knowledge (INDIC).
