Welcome to INDIC 2027

It is our great pleasure to welcome you to the vibrant and historic city of Jaipur, Rajasthan, for the 1st International Conference on Indian Knowledge Systems (INDIC 2027). Hosted by the University of Engineering & Management (UEM), Jaipur, the conference will be held from 02–03 April 2027, with both in-person and virtual participation options.

INDIC 2027 brings mathematicians, historians of science, Sanskritists, archaeologists and engineering faculty from India and abroad onto a shared, source-based evidentiary footing — building the first rigorous international forum dedicated to India's mathematical, astronomical and technological knowledge traditions.
