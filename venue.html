University of Engineering & Management (UEM) Jaipur: Shraddhavan Labhate Jnanam

UEM Jaipur is a premier NAAC-accredited, AICTE-approved research university situated on the scenic Sikar Road campus in Rajasthan. Committed to bridging ancient epistemologies with contemporary science and engineering, UEM serves as an active nodal center for Indian Knowledge Systems and technological innovation.

Jaipur as a Living Primary Laboratory: Founded in 1727 CE by the scholar-king Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, Jaipur is one of the world's most deliberate examples of town planning grounded in ancient Vāstu Śāstra and Siddhāntic astronomical alignment.

Just minutes from the conference venue stands the monumental Jantar Mantar (UNESCO World Heritage Site), housing nineteen massive architectural astronomical instruments—including the world's largest stone sundial (Vrihat Samrat Yantra). Conference participants will examine these operational masonry yantras alongside archival Sanskrit tables.
